5-Step Post-Clip Drill & 1–4 Scoring Rubric (Click to Expand) ▼

Live Session Rule: Play each clip once with no note-taking on the first listen. Then run these 5 steps aloud:

  1. Fact & Subtext Check: Core issue, emotional trigger, and what was Said vs. Implied vs. Unsaid.
  2. Intent & Emotion Tag: Agree on a 2-part tag (e.g., Intent: Reschedule / Emotion: Neutral).
  3. One-Sentence CRM Summary: 30-second write, then pick 2 trainees to read aloud.
  4. Live Agent Role-Play: Hot-seat immediate spoken reply graded on tone and accuracy.
  5. Peer Listening Rating: Class holds up 1–4 fingers and explains what was caught or missed.

1–4 Active Listening Scale:

  • 4 (Exemplary): Caught all explicit facts and implied/unstated concerns, matched tone, gave accurate next step.
  • 3 (Solid): Caught main facts and emotional tone, missed one secondary detail, gave clear next step.
  • 2 (Partial): Focused only on literal surface words, missed emotional driver or pivot, sounded robotic.
  • 1 (Missed): Misheard key facts (date/pest/address), ignored frustration, or jumped straight into policy.

1. Details & Subtext Recalls exact numbers/addresses and spots unspoken concerns (auto-pay threat, warranty fear). Confuses pest types or dates; repeats only the first sentence the caller said.
2. CRM Summary Captures who/where, what happened, and what is needed in one crisp sentence (<25 words). Writes a run-on paragraph or vague label ("Customer is mad about billing").
3. Intent & Emotion Names both functional goal and specific emotion (anxious, guarded, undervalued) + why. Labels every upset caller simply as "angry" or confuses emotion with request.
4. Agent Role-Play Leads with tailored empathy, mirrors key details, avoids excuses, asks one clear question. Uses canned apologies, blames dispatch/traffic, or re-asks info already given.
